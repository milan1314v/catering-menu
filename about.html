Our Journey

Reinventing How Events Discover Extraordinary Food

At Catering Menu, we believe food is the beating heart of every milestone celebration. Whether it is an intimate wedding banquet, an annual corporate summit, a festival live counter, or a milestone family reunion, the culinary experience creates memories that last for years.

Yet for decades, finding the right caterer has been stressful: opaque menus, hidden broker commissions, uncertainty about dietary capabilities, and endless telephone follow-ups. We created Catering Menu to change all of that - building a transparent, community-driven platform with verified caterer subdomains, complete menus, and authentic client reviews.
