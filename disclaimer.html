Legal Notice Last Updated: September 2026 4 min read

1. Directory Platform Role

Catering Menu (catering-menu.com) is an online discovery directory and information aggregation platform. We connect prospective event hosts, wedding couples, and corporate organizers with independent catering businesses, food trucks, and culinary providers.

Catering Menu is not a food preparation facility, kitchen, or direct catering provider. We do not prepare, cook, transport, or serve food items listed on this website.

2. Menu Accuracy, Ingredients & Pricing

All menus, package details, item descriptions, and service hours displayed on catering profile pages and subdomains are provided directly by the independent catering businesses or gathered from public resources.

Note on Seasonal Menus & Availability: Catering menus, market ingredient prices, and package inclusions are subject to change without prior notice by the respective caterer. We strongly advise clients to confirm all final menus, written estimates, and minimum guest count requirements directly with the caterer before signing booking contracts.

3. Food Allergies & Dietary Disclaimers

Clients with food allergies, celiac disease, strict religious dietary restrictions (Halal, Kosher), or medical requirements must communicate these directly in writing to the caterer:

  • Catering Menu cannot guarantee that food prepared by listed caterers is free from allergens including nuts, gluten, dairy, shellfish, eggs, or soy.
  • Cross-contamination risk in commercial catering kitchens is the sole responsibility of the individual catering provider.
  • Always request an allergen guarantee and ingredient disclosure directly from your selected caterer for any catered event.

4. Independent Caterer Contracts & Transactions

Any contract, booking agreement, payment, deposit, or cancellation policy entered into is strictly a direct bilateral agreement between you (the client) and the independent catering business.

  • Catering Menu is not a party to any catering agreement and holds no liability for disputes, cancellations, delays, or service dissatisfaction.
  • We do not collect deposits or processing fees on behalf of caterers.
  • Clients are encouraged to verify that their chosen caterer holds appropriate commercial liability insurance, health permits, and local business licenses.
