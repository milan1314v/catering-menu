Drop-off Catering: The caterer prepares and delivers ready-to-serve food in disposable platters or warmers without on-site staffing. Ideal for office lunches and casual parties.

Full-Service Catering: Includes on-site chefs, waitstaff, bartenders, setup, live cooking stations (e.g., carving tables, pasta bars), table clearing, and cleanup throughout your event.
