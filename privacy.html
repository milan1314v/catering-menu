Privacy Policy

Learn how Catering Menu protects, collects, and respects your personal and catering business data.

Official Policy Last Updated: September 2026 5 min read

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Catering Menu ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate catering-menu.com and its catering partner subdomains (e.g., *.catering-menu.com) as a premier discovery directory for catering businesses, event menus, and customer inquiries.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, explore caterer menus, leave customer reviews, or submit catering business listings.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide seamless catering discovery and booking inquiry facilitation:

  • Catering Business Profiles: Caterer name, culinary specialties, kitchen locations, operating hours, package descriptions, menus, and business contact information submitted via our "List Your Business" portal.
  • Customer Inquiries & Communications: Name, email address, event date, guest count, and message details when submitting inquiries or contact requests.
  • Customer Reviews & Ratings: Reviewer name, star rating, feedback text, and submission timestamp to ensure authentic community ratings.
  • Usage & Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, referral URLs, and interactions with search filters and live suggestions for service optimization and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate platform purposes:

  • To display catering services, menus, and verified locations accurately across main listings and individual caterer subdomains.
  • To forward direct catering quote requests and booking inquiries from event planners to listed caterers.
  • To facilitate search filtering by cuisine, event type (weddings, corporate, parties), and ratings.
  • To prevent fraudulent reviews, spam submissions, and unauthorized automated crawling.
  • To maintain and improve platform speed, security, and edge delivery via Cloudflare Pages and Workers.

4. Cookies & Analytical Technologies

We use minimal, privacy-focused cookies to enhance user experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for secure navigation, session security, and admin authentication.
  • Preference Cookies: Temporarily save your filter selections and search states during active browsing.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or client inquiry details to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We only share information under strict, transparent conditions:

  • With Caterers: When you send a booking inquiry or review, relevant details are delivered to the selected caterer so they can respond to your event request.
  • Service Providers: Secure infrastructure partners including Cloudflare (edge hosting, SSL encryption, and serverless compute).
  • Legal Compliance: When required by lawful judicial orders, subpoenas, or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

6. Your Rights & Data Control

You have full control over your data. Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, or local data protection laws), you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request immediate correction of inaccurate catering listings, menu pricing, or contact information.
  • Request deletion of your business listing, submitted reviews, or inquiry history from our database.
  • Opt out of any marketing or platform updates at any time.
